Kill the Hero

Website 0.01

Kill the Hero is a web-based Trading Card Game (TCG) that combines collectible card mechanics and strategic board-based movement. Players compete by advancing their hero across a shared board, playing Terrain, Equipment, and Villainy cards to strengthen themselves or hinder their opponent. Victory is achieved either by defeating the villain at the end of the board or killing the opposing hero.

Features

Strategic Board Movement

Advance your hero across a shared board, making tactical decisions about movement and positioning to outmaneuver your opponent.

Diverse Card Types

Deploy powerful Terrain, Equipment, and Villainy cards to strengthen your hero or hinder your opponent's progress.

Collectible Cards

Build and customize your deck from a vast collection of cards, each with unique abilities and strategic value.

Multiple Victory Paths

Defeat the villain at the end of the board or eliminate your opponent's hero. Choose your own path to victory.

PvP Gameplay

Compete against players worldwide in real-time matches with dynamic, strategic card battles.

Getting Started

Jump into the action in just a few simple steps

1

Create Account

Sign up with your email or choose a username to get started. Your account keeps your progress safe.

2

Select Your Hero

Choose from unique heroes like Riptide or Elpis. Your hero determines your starter deck and special abilities.

3

Build Your Deck

Customize your deck with cards from your collection. Add Terrain, Equipment, and Villainy cards to your strategy.

4

Enter Battle

Face off against opponents in real-time PvP matches. Prove your tactical skills and earn rewards!
